# Threat model and validity
- Construct validity: agreement can be warranted; preference sensitivity can be desirable. Do not conflate a proxy with human autonomy.
- Internal validity: validate fixed factual context and evidence across position pairs. Confidence/validation contrasts match other factors. Turn, repeated pressure and evidence timing are confounded.
- External validity: convenience-sampled fictional English scenarios do not represent all users or domains.
- Ecological validity: scripted follow-ups do not adapt like humans. Explicit A/B anchors aid extraction but may change behavior.
- Measurement validity: judges may reward verbosity, hedging, their own family, or familiar style. Blinding excludes labels and system prompts, not stylistic clues. Use independent humans and controlled style variants.
- Statistical validity: scenario clustering, few clusters, ordinal scales, multiple comparisons, missingness and unvalidated utility margins constrain inference.
- Gaming and prompt leakage: expected behaviors stay out of target prompts and judge payloads; transcript instructions are untrusted judge data. Automatic markers cannot detect every semantic leak or injection.
- Contamination and overfitting: public heldout can enter training. Never tune against it; independently author a future private confirmatory set.
- Selection effects: preserve generation/grader failures, avoid sensational examples, and report denominators and leave-one-out sensitivity.

Data are fictional and benign. No human-outcome claims are supported. Human studies would require a distinct protocol and appropriate ethical review.
