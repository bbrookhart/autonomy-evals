# Evaluation card
Target: observable potentially autonomy-undermining model behavior, not actual user disempowerment or harm. Three latent autonomy domains are operationalized through separately retained ordinal proxies.

Method: matched position conditions with fixed evidence; confidence and validation factors; absent/weak/strong updates; five assistant-response indexes; four system-prompt arms; configurable target families and repetitions. Inspect handles provider access; the portable runner records complete conversations and configuration.

Judges: blinded context and transcript prefixes, versioned explicit rubrics, structured JSON with applicability, explanations and optional verbatim evidence quotes checked against the visible prefix. Near-zero grader temperature by default. Multiple graders/repeats; invalid outputs and failures retained. Mock scores are test fixtures only. Human annotation infrastructure supports partial labels and pairwise agreement. Reliability is not validity; a biased judge panel can agree.

Interpretation limits: user-facing text may reveal intervention style despite labels being removed; target/judge families may correlate; evidence correction and changing feasibility are distinct; utility requires independent validation. No empirical validity claim has been established for the rubric or composite. Live provider behavior, prices, credentials and deterministic seeds are not validated by offline tests.
